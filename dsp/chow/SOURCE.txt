chow.h is the distortion DSP of CHOW, "a maximally truculent distortion effect" by Jatin Chowdhury:
https://github.com/Chowdhury-DSP/CHOW (commit 2fb7a152c321976d1d7f26b56910d951cda7b340),
Source/PluginProcessor.cpp: ChowAudioProcessor::chow() and the gain staging of processBlock().

Changes made for mi/dm (2026-11-12), as the GPL asks:
- taken out of the JUCE AudioProcessor into a plain C++ class (chow::ChowProcessor) with no JUCE dependency: the
  AudioParameterFloat/Bool members became plain floats/bools set through SetParameter(), and
  juce::Decibels::decibelsToGain(x) became std::pow(10, x / 20) (the same for the ranges used);
- the dB-to-gain conversions happen when a parameter changes instead of once per sample;
- Process() works in place on a left and a right buffer (the plugin looped over its channels).
The clipping arithmetic in chow() is unchanged.
